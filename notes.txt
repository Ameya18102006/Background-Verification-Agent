Design implications
Run Tier 1 first and short-circuit on a hit. A sanctions match changes the whole report.
Use domain profiles to reweight, not just to select. For example, license status jumps to Tier 1 for hiring a contractor, and ownership links jump to Tier 1 for partnerships.
Combine severity with confidence. A weak source such as reviews should never outrank an official record, even when the flag sounds alarming.

The site filter isn't reliable: the Indian Kanoon hits are real judgments, but the sebi.gov.in and sec.gov searches returned Reddit threads and review pages labelled as official. I'm testing how to detect that kind of mislabeling next.

Score = severity × how much this flag matters in this domain (tier) × source reliability (official record > data-provider record > news > web page).
